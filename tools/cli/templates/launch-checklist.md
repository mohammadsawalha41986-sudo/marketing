## Go / no-go

The launch lead makes the call three business days out. Any unchecked blocker
below is an automatic no-go until it is either fixed or explicitly waived.

- [ ] Feature is on in production for all target accounts
- [ ] Pricing and packaging signed off
- [ ] Support has the runbook and knows the escalation path
- [ ] Legal review complete for external claims
- [ ] Rollback plan written

## Four weeks out

- [ ] Positioning and messaging approved
- [ ] Naming final
- [ ] Customer proof identified — at least one named reference
- [ ] Analyst and press list drafted
- [ ] Landing page brief written

## Two weeks out

- [ ] Landing page in staging
- [ ] Launch blog drafted
- [ ] Demo recorded
- [ ] Sales one-pager and objection handling drafted
- [ ] Email sequence built for the installed base
- [ ] Social assets designed
- [ ] Paid campaigns built, paused

## One week out

- [ ] Sales enablement session delivered
- [ ] Support macros updated
- [ ] Docs published behind the flag
- [ ] Tracking verified end to end — UTMs, conversion events, CRM fields
- [ ] Press release approved

## Launch day

- [ ] Feature flag on
- [ ] Landing page live
- [ ] Blog published
- [ ] Emails sent
- [ ] Social posted
- [ ] Paid campaigns unpaused
- [ ] Partner and community posts live
- [ ] Team watching the inbox and social for the first four hours

## First week after

- [ ] Daily check on conversion and support volume
- [ ] Objections collected from sales calls
- [ ] Messaging adjusted where it is not landing

## Thirty days after

- [ ] Results against targets written up
- [ ] What we would do differently, recorded here
- [ ] Assets archived or retired
