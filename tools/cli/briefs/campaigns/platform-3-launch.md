## The one-sentence goal

Generate 450 MQLs and $1.1M of pipeline between 17 August and 30 September by
making 3.0 the obvious choice for teams whose legacy suite renewal is inside
twelve months.

## Why now

Two things converge. 3.0 is the first release where a process owner can change a
governed workflow without us in the room, which is the objection that has cost us
the most enterprise deals this year. And the two largest legacy suites in our
category renew on calendar-year cycles, so September is when transformation
budgets for next year get argued about.

Waiting a quarter means arguing about a budget that is already allocated.

## Who it is for

- **Segment:** `ops-scaleup` (primary), `enterprise-transform` (secondary)
- **Buying role:** Director of Business Systems is the champion. They are
  accountable for change requests they cannot currently service, and 3.0 is
  aimed squarely at that.
- **Trigger:** a renewal conversation with an incumbent, or a change request that
  has been queued for more than a quarter.
- **Current alternative:** wait for the incumbent's professional services team, or
  route the process around the tool via spreadsheets.

## What we are claiming

- **Promise:** the team that owns the process changes it, the same week, with
  versioning and rollback.
- **Proof:** median first workflow live in nine days (Q2 2026 onboarding cohort);
  Meridian cut cycle time 38% in one quarter.
- **Objection we expect:** *"Our governance team will never allow business users
  to edit production workflows."* Honest answer: they should not, and 3.0 does
  not ask them to — edits are versioned, reviewed and rolled back like code, and
  the approval path maps to the org chart. That is the demo's opening five
  minutes, not a footnote.

## Offer and next step

- **Offer:** a 30-minute working session where the prospect brings their most
  awkward workflow and we build it live.
- **Call to action:** "Bring your worst workflow." One CTA on every surface.
- **Where it lands:** `/platform-3` — a single page carrying the demo video, the
  governance explanation, and the booking form.

## What would make us stop

If by 12 September we are under 150 MQLs and under $300k of pipeline, we stop the
paid spend and hold the remaining $40k for the competitive displacement campaign
in Q4. That is roughly a third of the way to target at two thirds of the window,
and past that point the pattern is a positioning problem that more budget will
not fix.
